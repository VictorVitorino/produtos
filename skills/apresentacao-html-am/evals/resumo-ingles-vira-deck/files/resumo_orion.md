# Orion Foods — supply chain diagnostic (summary for leadership)

**Scope.** Eight weeks, four plants (Dayton, Fresno, Joliet, Macon), two distribution centers, 2025 data.

**Headline.** Orion spends **US$ 412M a year** on supply chain operations. We see **US$ 38–52M** of annual savings potential (9–13%), most of it in transportation and inventory.

**Where the money goes (2025).**
- Transportation: US$ 164M (40%) — 58% of loads are booked spot, against an industry benchmark of 20–25%.
- Warehousing: US$ 103M (25%) — utilization of 61% in the two DCs.
- Inventory carrying cost: US$ 86M (21%) — 47 days of inventory vs. 32-day target.
- Procurement of packaging and MRO: US$ 59M (14%) — 1,230 active suppliers.

**Root causes.** No single owner of the S&OP process; forecast accuracy of 64% at SKU level; plants plan transport independently; supplier base never rationalized since the 2021 merger.

**Levers and potential.**
1. Transport tendering and load consolidation: US$ 16–21M.
2. Inventory policy and S&OP redesign: US$ 11–15M.
3. DC network and slotting: US$ 6–9M.
4. Supplier consolidation (packaging, MRO): US$ 5–7M.

**Roadmap.** Wave 1 (months 1–3): transport tender and quick wins. Wave 2 (months 4–8): S&OP redesign and inventory policy. Wave 3 (months 9–12): DC network decision and supplier consolidation.

**Asks to leadership.** Appoint an S&OP owner reporting to the COO; approve the transport tender in Q1; fund a 12-week PMO.
